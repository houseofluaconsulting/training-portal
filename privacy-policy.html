Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 19, 2025

This privacy notice for Whammy Enterprises LLC (doing business as https://redrock-financial.com) (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) describes how and why we might collect, store, use, and/or share (“process”) your information when you use our services (“Services”), such as when you:

  • Visit our website at https://redrock-financial.com
  • Interact with us through Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Messenger)
  • Submit your information via forms, including lead generation forms
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including marketing, events, or customer service

If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at support@redrock-financial.com.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

We may collect the following information from you:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location (e.g. ZIP code, state)
  • Any other personal data you choose to provide

We collect this data when you:

  • Submit a form on our website or Meta lead ad
  • Contact us directly via email, phone, or social media
  • Interact with our ads or website

2. HOW DO WE USE YOUR INFORMATION?

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries or requests
  • Provide quotes, consultations, or services
  • Follow up via phone, text, or email
  • Analyze engagement and improve our Services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

3. WILL YOUR INFORMATION BE SHARED?

We may share your information with:

  • Internal employees or consultants for follow-up
  • Service providers (e.g. CRM, email/text platforms, analytics tools)
  • Legal authorities when required by law

4. HOW DO WE STORE AND PROTECT YOUR DATA?

We take data security seriously. Your information is stored using industry-standard security practices, and only authorized personnel have access to it.

However, no electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. Use the Services at your own risk.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access or request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of future communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@redrock-financial.com.

6. DO-NOT-TRACK & AD TRACKING

We may use cookies and tracking technologies to improve your experience and run targeted ads. You can adjust your browser settings to control these technologies.

We currently do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals due to a lack of industry standard.

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. CHANGES TO THIS NOTICE

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at https://redrock-financial.com/privacy. Continued use of our Services constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
