Carrier Specifics - Contracting Information

NLG, F&G, Royal Neighbors, & Athene

In order to get contracted with NLG, F&G, Royal Neighbors (RN) & Athene there are a few important details new agents should know:

  1. Two trainings are required if an agent wants to get contracted with these carriers. The annuity & IUL trainings under training camp on Gateway
    2. You can get to Gateway a few different ways but here is how to locate these 2 trainings:
    • Log into HCMS account. In the top right-hand corner there is an icon that looks like a group of squares. Click that icon.
    • Then click Gateway. A new webpage will probably be loaded for Gateway.
    • On the left-hand side of the page (the tabs/navigation bar), click trainings camp. The IUL & Annuity trainings will be on that page.
  2. After completing the IUL & Annuity training reach out to your admin (Allie) and your admin will need to email corporate to request contracting for the 4 carriers listed above.
    • Corporate will then send a contracting link that will need to be signed and submitted through Surancebay

Ethos

Ethos contracting is different from other insurance carriers & the process can be slightly confusing.
  • With Ethos, every agent automatically has a profile and the ability to contract with them.
  • Ethos creates agent portals automatically after receiving bi-weekly reports of new agents. Please allow 6-8 weeks from onboarding into HCMS for this portal to be created.

After the 6-8 week period agents need to create/sign into their profile using the link below.
https://agents.ethoslife.com/login

Below is a screenshot of the page you will be directed to after clicking the link.
  • DO NOT CLICK SIGN UP (this will put you direct to Ethos which is not what we want)
    • If an agent runs into the situation where they did SIGN UP, they will need to notify their admin.
  • After accessing the Ethos links agents will need to login using number or email
  • Once that is taken care of, Ethos is notified and agents should receive a welcome email
If you receive an error after attempting to log-in using your number or email please notify your admin.
Ethos - IUL/Ameritas, Banner Life
If an agent is needing to access/utilize Ethos’s Ameritas/IUL, Banner Life products check the following:
  • First check through your dashboard to see if Ameritas/IUL is available for you to quote or start and application for a client
  • If you do not have the quoting or application available, log into your NLC account and submit a contracting request for ETHOS.
  • Agents will then sign and submit the contract in surancebay.
  • Once that is approved, agents can use the same Ethos link to login (https://agents.ethoslife.com/login) and the Ameritas/IUL product should be available.
