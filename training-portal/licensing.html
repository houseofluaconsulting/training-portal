Licensing/Additional Licenses

Applying for State License

After contracting is completed connect with your admin (Allie Goodman) about applying for more state licensing. You will then follow the steps below.

Agents will need to apply for at least 7 state licenses.
You don’t have to do these all at once but the goal is to get those applications completed within a reasonable amount of time.

How to apply for a new license:

You will visit NIPR’s website to apply for a new license:
https://nipr.com/licensing-center/apply

Click on Licensing Center on the navigation bar

Click on Apply for a License and select Non-resident

Then you will follow the prompted steps

NOTE: When applying to some states, it can be a little confusing in terms of what to select or how to answer a question.
Please reference this document for clarification: State Specicfic Details
